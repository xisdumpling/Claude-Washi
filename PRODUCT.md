# Product
## Name
Claude Washi (for Obsidian)

## Register

product

## Users

Obsidian users who want a calm, readable workspace for sustained note taking, study, and technical writing.

## Product Purpose

Adapt Claude Desktop's visual language to Obsidian without weakening familiar Obsidian workflows, information density, or plugin compatibility.

## Brand Personality

Calm, restrained, precise.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboard styling, excessive cards, loud inactive states, overlapping native controls, and visual changes that obscure standard Obsidian affordances.

## Design Principles

- Keep the interface subordinate to reading and writing.
- Preserve clear ownership between native window chrome and Obsidian panels.
- Reuse the established typography, warm-neutral surfaces, and terracotta accent tokens.
- Prefer small, targeted compatibility rules over broad DOM overrides.
- Verify changes in the running app across desktop, narrow-window, light, and dark states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, responsive overflow behavior, and usable controls at narrow widths.
